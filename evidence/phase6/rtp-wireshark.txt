Timing and intentional-silence observation
-----------------------------------------
The operator confirmed deliberate silence for approximately seconds
60-75 of the test after two-way speech had been heard earlier. In the
observed B -> A stream, the packet table contains additional startup
and mid-call pauses. Near the end of the capture, overlapping the operator-reported
silent interval, notable successive gaps include roughly 1.32 s
followed later by two approximately 5 s gaps. The latter two show about 40000 RTP ticks
of timestamp advance each at the 8000 Hz media clock; sequence numbers
remain continuous and a marker appears on the first packet after each
gap. The opposite selected A -> B stream continued near-20-ms packet
transmission during the same portion of the test.

The observations are compatible with silence suppression or a related
media-pause mechanism on one observed stream; they do not prove a
particular feature, vendor setting, or which sender deliberately muted
transmission. In particular, 15 s of user silence should NOT be
reported as one unbroken 15 s packet gap. Zero lost packets means no
loss detected within these analyzed streams, NOT uninterrupted media,
no audible impairment, or zero loss elsewhere in the network.

The opposite stream's 21.954884-ms "maximum delta" is Wireshark's
summary measure; larger marker-delimited gaps are visible in the
packet table and must be documented separately.

Manual and data-handling notes
------------------------------
Audibility is based on the operator's report, not an inference from RTP
counters. This wire-level outgoing-call study is separate from the earlier
incoming/outgoing application acceptance gates. Keep the original
packet capture private outside the repository. Never commit captures, unredacted
screenshots, PCMU payload bytes, raw SIP logs, or credentials.
See docs/protocols/rtp.md for interpretation and limitations.
