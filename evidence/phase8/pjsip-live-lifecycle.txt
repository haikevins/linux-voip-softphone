The observed sequence is real runtime evidence that PJSUA2 2.17 returned
PJ_EBUSY for shutdown2(force=false) while a call slot was active, and that a
retry succeeded after hangup, DISCONNECTED callback completion, and zero call
slots. It is not a universal callback-quiescence proof and it did not inject
PJ_EBUSY through the production SipIncomingCallClient/SipOutgoingCallClient
stop() methods. The probe counters cover `ProbeAccount::onRegState` registration callbacks.
No late registration callback was observed at shutdown return, during the
1.5-second grace interval, or through libDestroy() in this recorded run. That
absence is an observation, not a vendor-wide lifetime guarantee and not proof
that every possible PJSUA2 account callback source was quiescent.
