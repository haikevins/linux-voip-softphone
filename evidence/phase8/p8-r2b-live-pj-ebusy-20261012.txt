## Interpretation and boundaries

- The `VOIP_PHASE8_R2B_LAB`-guarded entry point invokes the existing
  production `SipOutgoingCallClient::shutdown_account_safely()` on its actual
  owned `Account`, while `TrackedCall` and a PJSUA2 call slot are present.
  `PJ_EBUSY` is checked against the real `pj::Error::status`; it is not injected.
- `OWNERS_RETAINED=PASS` checks Account/Endpoint pointer identity, Account
  validity, and presence of `TrackedCall` immediately after the early failure.
  It is not a proof that all vendor callbacks have become quiescent.
- `STOP_RETRY=RETURNED` records the normal production `stop()` returning
  following hangup and `DISCONNECTED`; this is not an independent measurement
  of the vendor status code from the later `shutdown2(false)` call. Successful
  same-object restart and registration additionally support completed cleanup
  within the tested sequence.
- The lab does not establish that the target answered, that normal `stop()`
  encountered `PJ_EBUSY`, that `SipIncomingCallClient` or
  `SipRegistrationClient` recovered, or that every potential shutdown race is
  safe. There was only one live run; no automatic retries were performed.
