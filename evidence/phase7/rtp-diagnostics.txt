Phase 7 live RTP/audio diagnostics — sanitized representative samples
====================================================================

Purpose
-------
Record representative values observed while validating the Qt `Live RTP / Audio` panel. These are snapshots from the local lab, not performance guarantees.

Incoming/answered call sample
-----------------------------
Negotiated audio: PCMU @ 8 kHz, sendrecv
Observed call time at teardown: approximately 50 seconds
RX: approximately 2.4K packets, 394.2 KB payload accounting
TX: approximately 2.2K packets, 367.3 KB payload accounting
Reported receive packet loss: 0 (0.0%)
Receive jitter: average approximately 1.246 ms, max approximately 6.750 ms
RTT: average approximately 0.659 ms, max approximately 1.205 ms

Outgoing/reuse call sample
--------------------------
Negotiated audio: PCMU @ 8 kHz, sendrecv
Observed call time at snapshot: approximately 13 seconds
RX: 640 packets, 102.4 KB payload accounting
TX: 698 packets, 111.6 KB payload accounting
Reported receive packet loss: 0 (0.0%)
Receive jitter: average approximately 0.347 ms, max approximately 0.625 ms
RTT: average approximately 0.694 ms, max approximately 1.037 ms

UI behavior
-----------
The operator confirmed that RX/TX counters increased while speaking, codec/loss/jitter/RTT were displayed, the panel disappeared after teardown, and a later call on the same registration published a fresh snapshot.

Limitations
-----------
These are application/PJSUA2 runtime snapshots, not a replacement for packet-level Wireshark analysis. Zero reported loss in these samples does not establish zero loss for all calls, all streams, or other networks.

Security/redaction
------------------
Peer IP addresses, ports, raw SIP headers, credentials, and audio payloads are omitted.
