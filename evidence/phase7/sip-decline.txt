Phase 7 SIP Decline semantics — sanitized observation
====================================================

Purpose
-------
Verify which SIP status is emitted by the Qt/PJSUA2 softphone when the user presses Decline on an unanswered incoming call.

Observed call-leg sequence
--------------------------
1. Asterisk sent an INVITE to the registered 1002 softphone.
2. The softphone returned 100 Trying and 180 Ringing during normal incoming-call setup.
3. After the user pressed Decline, Asterisk received:

   SIP/2.0 603 Decline

   from the 1002 softphone on the callee-side dialog.

4. Asterisk ACKed that final response, mapped the dial result internally, and then sent the 1001 caller on the separate caller-side dialog:

   SIP/2.0 403 Forbidden
   Reason: Q.850;cause=21

Conclusion
----------
The Phase 7 client implements explicit SIP 603 Decline correctly. The Redmi/Linphone UI can still display 403 because Asterisk is a B2BUA and generated a separate final response on the caller-facing leg.

Security/redaction
------------------
This summary intentionally omits Call-ID values, transport ports, Digest Authorization material, SIP credentials, and full raw headers.
